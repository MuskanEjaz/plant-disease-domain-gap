# Literature survey matrix (verified references)

Each entry was checked against Crossref DOI metadata, arXiv/PMLR, or the paper's own full text, as noted. The numbers come from the papers themselves unless the entry says otherwise.

| # | Reference (IEEE style) | Datasets / setup | Main finding | Relevance to our experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D. P. Hughes and M. Salathé, "An open access repository of images on plant health to enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics," arXiv:1511.08060, 2015. | PlantVillage release: 54,309 images, 14 crops. Detached leaves on a grey/black sheet, photographed with one camera, then cropped and rotated. | Introduces the open PlantVillage repository. | The uniform capture protocol is the source of the lab-domain bias we measure. |
| 2 | S. P. Mohanty, D. P. Hughes, and M. Salathé, "Using deep learning for image-based plant disease detection," *Front. Plant Sci.*, vol. 7, Art. no. 1419, 2016, doi: 10.3389/fpls.2016.01419. | PlantVillage, 54,306 images, 38 classes. AlexNet/GoogLeNet, transfer vs. scratch, 60 configurations. | 99.35% held-out accuracy, but only 31.40% / 31.69% on two small sets of web/IPM images. | The first report of the lab-to-field collapse, matching our 92.08% → 34.89% macro-F1. |
| 3 | D. Singh, N. Jain, P. Jain, P. Kayal, S. Kumawat, and N. Batra, "PlantDoc: A dataset for visual plant disease detection," in *Proc. 7th ACM IKDD CoDS and 25th COMAD*, 2020, pp. 249–253, doi: 10.1145/3371158.3371196. | PlantDoc: 2,598 field images, 13 species, 27 classes. VGG16, InceptionV3, InceptionResNetV2, with and without PlantVillage pre-training. | PlantVillage → PlantDoc (full images, VGG16): 15.08%. PlantVillage → cropped PlantDoc: 19.73–39.87%. Fine-tuning on cropped PlantDoc: up to 70.53%. | The closest prior work. Our field fine-tuning repeats its protocol with stricter leakage control. |
| 4 | K. P. Ferentinos, "Deep learning models for plant disease detection and diagnosis," *Comput. Electron. **Notes**
- PlantVillage size: 54,306 images (Mohanty) vs. 54,309 (Hughes). Each figure is cited with its own source.
- PlantDoc's abstract says "up to 31%". The table difference is +30.66 points (39.87% → 70.53%), and the report cites the table values.
- Boulent et al. [13] is listed only because it is the source of the Ferentinos lab/field numbers; it was not verified separately here. Check those numbers against the Ferentinos PDF before final submission if library access is available.
